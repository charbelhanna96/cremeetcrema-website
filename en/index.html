FAQ

Do you need a special power outlet?

No. Our cart runs on a standard 120 V outlet. We bring our own heavy-duty extension cord.

Do you need running water?

No. We bring our own water and take our wastewater with us.

Do you have dairy-free options?

Yes. You choose your milks, and we can accommodate any type of milk based on your preferences (oat, almond, soy, lactose-free…).

Do you accommodate smaller events?

Absolutely! Whether it's an intimate gathering or a large celebration, we'd love to hear about it. Every event is quoted individually based on your needs, and minimum booking charges may apply.

How much space do you need?

A small spot indoors or under cover, near an outlet. We confirm the details with you before the event.

How do I book?

Fill out the quote form. A deposit confirms your date.
